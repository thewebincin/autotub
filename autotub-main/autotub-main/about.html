Our Story

Founded with an obsessive passion for cars and an uncompromising standard for cleanliness, Auto Tub was born out of a simple frustration: paying premium prices for basic washes every single time a car got dirty. We realized Trivandrum deserved better.

We introduced the Unlimited Wash Model. Our mission became clear—give car owners the absolute best detailing, state-of-the-art washing technology, and uncompromised VIP care, all neatly packaged into flat-rate, worry-free plans. A clean car is a happy car, and an unlimited wash means your car is always happy.
